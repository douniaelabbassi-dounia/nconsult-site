Our assignments

Targeted interventions or transformation programmes

Whether you need a one-off insight or long-term support, we build a response suited to your maturity and your challenges.

Procurement assessment

Evaluate the maturity of the Procurement function and identify the priority levers for creating value.

Strategy & organisation

Define a Procurement policy aligned with company strategy and a suitable organisation.

Spend mapping

Make spending visible to decide better and focus efforts where they matter.

  • Supplier risk management

Supply Chain & operational excellence

  • Continuous process improvement
