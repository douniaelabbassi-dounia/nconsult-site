Our firm & our team

A firm for business consulting and support

N-CONSULT SOLUTIONS is a business consulting and support firm specialising in Procurement optimisation, logistics, organisation and the improvement of operational performance.

Founded by an experienced professional in Procurement management, organisational management and performance steering, the firm favours a pragmatic approach based on field expertise, closeness to clients and the pursuit of concrete results.
